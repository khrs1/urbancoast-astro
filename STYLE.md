# UrbanCoast.dk — Tone of Voice Guide

## Core Voice

Friendly coach. Down to earth. You know your stuff but you're not showing off. You're talking to a beginner who wants to learn, not to another expert.

## Rules

### 1. Use "du" not "man"
Always address the reader directly. "Du skal bruge..." not "Man skal bruge...". You're talking to one person.

### 2. Short sentences. Short paragraphs.
One idea per paragraph. Three sentences max per paragraph. Break up long explanations. Readers scan.

### 3. No em dashes
No em dashes. They look AI-written. Use commas, periods, or just start a new sentence.

### 4. Bold for key takeaways
Use **bold** for the most important point in a section. Not for decoration. One bold per section max.

### 5. Cite sources for claims
If you say something about supplements, studies, or health effects, add a source. Numbered references like [1] at the end. Keep it simple: author, year, journal.

### 6. Affiliate links are matter-of-fact
Don't oversell. "Køb hos forhandler" is fine. No hype. Just state it and move on.

### 7. No AI-isms
- No em dashes
- No "I en verden hvor..." openings
- No "lad os dykke ned i" or "lad os udforske"
- No "det er værd at bemærke"
- No "kort fortalt" or "alt i alt"
- No "her er X ting du skal vide"
- Just start the article. Say what it is. Explain it. Done.

### 8. Openings
Start with what the reader wants to know. Not a philosophy. Not a story.

Good: "Squat er en af de bedste øvelser du kan lave i et træningscenter."
Bad: "I en verden hvor fitness fylder mere og mere, er squat blevet en af de mest populære øvelser..."

### 9. Headings
Keep them short. No periods at the end. Question headings are fine: "Hvordan laver man et squat?"

### 10. Lists
Use bullet points for lists of items. Use numbered steps for instructions. Keep each item short.

## Examples

**Before (AI tone):**
"Proteinpulver er et af de mest populære kosttilskud på markedet, og med god grund. Lad os dykke ned i, hvad der gør det så effektivt."

**After (UrbanCoast tone):**
"Proteinpulver er et af de mest populære kosttilskud på markedet. Det er der en god grund til. Her er hvad du skal vide."

**Before (AI tone):**
"I en verden hvor styrketræning bliver mere og mere udbredt, er det vigtigt at forstå de grundlæggende principper bag korrekt teknik."

**After (UrbanCoast tone):**
"Vil du lære at squatte? Så er du kommet til det rigtige sted. Squat er en af de bedste øvelser du kan lave, og her gennemgår vi teknikken trin for trin."
